How I approach technology

  1. 01

    Understand the service

    Before troubleshooting infrastructure, understand the service, its dependencies, its users and its business impact.

  2. 02

    Protect identity and access

    Identity, authentication and authorization are fundamental security boundaries across modern enterprise environments.

  3. 03

    Observe the environment

    Monitoring and useful operational visibility allow teams to detect degradation before it becomes a larger business impact.

  4. 04

    Respond systematically

    Effective incident management requires prioritization, communication, escalation and technical discipline.

  5. 05

    Learn from failure

    Root Cause Analysis and post-incident reviews should lead to measurable improvements rather than simply closing tickets.

  6. 06

    Automate what can be repeated

    Repetitive operational work is an opportunity for automation, consistency and better engineering practices.
